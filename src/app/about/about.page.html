About SortiFy What is SortiFy?

SortiFy is your smart recycling assistant. We help users learn about waste sorting, track recycling habits, and engage in green community projects. We're all about saving the environment — and saving money while doing it.

Our mobile app uses real-time GPS to guide users to nearby waste bins, recycling centers, and eco-drop points based on the type of waste they need to dispose of. With personalized tips, smart sorting suggestions, and support for rewards, we’re not just helping you throw stuff away — we’re helping you sort it forward.

The Birth of SortiFy

Born out of a university project, Sortify started as a way to reduce waste mismanagement in our own neighborhoods. What began as a prototype has grown into a full-fledged platform empowering people across the country.

Why It Matters

Sortify solves the real problem of poor waste management by making education, reporting, and community involvement easy. It reduces waste build-up, encourages recycling, and bridges the gap between communities and waste management services.
